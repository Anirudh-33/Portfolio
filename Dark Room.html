UNITY IN CREATIVITY

The event was organized in the year 2022, where people participated in various competitions from all across India. Anirudh, focusing on expressing rather than winning, wrote a poem in simple words keeping in mind to include various figures of speech and tints of every possible national attribute people are proud of.

The title was — This is the Nation I was Born Into, a patriotic poem submitted for Deshbhakti Geet Competition.

Next year, in February, the results were announced and Anirudh secured second position at state level (U.P.), winning ₹75,000 and recognition from Mrs. Meenakshi Lekhi.
